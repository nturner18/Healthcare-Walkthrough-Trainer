# Healthcare Walkthrough Trainer

A browser-based practice tool for the 30-minute security walkthroughs AegisPro CyberShield TX runs for small healthcare practices. You walk through a fictional dental office, Brightline Family Dental, spot security and compliance issues, and practice how to raise them with the owner.

## How a run works
- Each run switches on 12 to 15 of 26 possible findings and shows the rest in their fixed state, so no two runs are the same. Patient names, passwords and card numbers also change every run.
- Five rooms: reception, hallway, operatory, imaging room and back office.
- Tap anything you would bring up. Decide whether to raise it, how severe it is, and whether to give the fix away or save it for the assessment.
- Each finding includes a talk track, the full recommendation, an owner pushback with a response, a question to ask, and a HIPAA, PCI DSS or Texas reference.
- The debrief separates findings you found on your own from ones found with a hint, revealed, found after time, or raised on a second look. It also covers password patterns, which three findings to lead with, an SB 2610 closer, and professional conduct.

## Settings
- 30-minute clock
- Realistic mode, which hides how many findings there are
- Office sounds

## Running it
Use the live version at https://nturner18.github.io/Healthcare-Walkthrough-Trainer/ or open index.html in any modern browser. Nothing to install. Progress saves in your browser.

## Disclaimer
The practice, staff and patients are fictional. Findings reference HIPAA, PCI DSS v4.0.1 and Texas law for training purposes only. This is not legal advice or a compliance certification.
