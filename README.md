# AegisPro Walkthrough Trainer

A browser-based practice tool for the 30-minute security walkthroughs AegisPro CyberShield TX runs for small businesses. Pick a scenario, walk through a fictional business, spot security and compliance issues, and practice how to raise them with the owner.

## Scenarios

* Dental practice (Brightline Family Dental): five rooms and 26 possible findings, with HIPAA, PCI DSS and Texas law references.
* Restaurant (Pecan Street Kitchen): a full-service restaurant with a bar, five areas and 24 possible findings, led by PCI DSS, with Texas Chapter 521 and SB 2610 references.

## How a run works

* Each run switches on 12 to 15 findings and shows the rest in their fixed state, so no two runs are the same. Names, passwords and card numbers also change every run.
* Tap anything you would bring up. Decide whether to raise it, how severe it is, and whether to give the fix away or save it for the assessment.
* Each finding includes a talk track, the full recommendation, an owner pushback with a response, a question to ask, and a reference.
* Professional conduct counts: get the walkthrough authorization signed, and resist the temptations to touch what you should only look at.
* The debrief separates findings you found on your own from ones found with a hint, revealed, found after time, or raised on a second look. It also covers patterns worth connecting, which three findings to lead with, an SB 2610 closer, and professional conduct.

## Settings

* Scenario
* 30-minute clock
* Realistic mode, which hides how many findings there are
* Background sounds

## Running it

Use the live version at https://nturner18.github.io/Healthcare-Walkthrough-Trainer/ or open index.html in any modern browser. Nothing to install. Progress saves in your browser.
